Available Tie-breaks

DE – Direct Encounter: score among tied players who played each other.

Buchholz Cut 1: sum of opponents' scores after dropping the lowest opponent score.

Buchholz: sum of opponents' final scores.

Sonneborn-Berger: opponent score weighted by your result against that opponent.

Wins: number of wins.

Black Wins: wins scored with Black.

Cumulative: sum of your running score after each round.

Rating: starting rating as a final local fallback.

About the Swiss Engine

This app is designed for school, club, classroom, and local-event management. BUILD 12 adds hard repeat-opponent and duplicate-bye safeguards, but the built-in Swiss routine remains a practical offline local-event engine rather than a claim of FIDE endorsement. For formally FIDE-rated Swiss events, verify pairings with the appropriate published/endorsed system and software required by the event regulations.
