Chess Tournament Manager v2.0.3.1 startup fix

Fixes:
- Does not copy the bundled app out of app.asar on first startup.
- Loads bundled app directly until a two-file runtime update is installed.
- Disables hardware acceleration for broader Windows compatibility.
- Removes silent single-instance exit behavior.
- Writes startup diagnostics to:
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Chess Tournament Manager\startup.log

The MATH-a-PANG-style two-file updater remains enabled.
